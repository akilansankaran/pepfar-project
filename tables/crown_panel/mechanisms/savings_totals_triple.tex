HIV prevalence is among ages 15--49 in 2003 (percentage points); PEPFAR is the 0/1 group dummy (All PEPFAR or COP, by column). The coefficient is how much more the post-2004 change in the outcome rises per percentage point of HIV prevalence in the treatment group than in the controls. Adjusted columns add the other 10 baseline (2003) covariates of Crown et al.\ (2023), each interacted with Post. Outcomes from the World Bank WDI (gross savings NY.GNS.ICTR.CD, gross fixed capital formation NE.GDI.FTOT.CD), in current US\$ deflated to 2022 dollars with the US GDP deflator. Totals are per-person values times UN WPP population; Raw and Winsorized rows in billions of dollars, IHS of the winsorized value in dollars. Winsorized: values outside the 1st--99th percentiles of the full panel set to those percentiles. IHS: $\sinh^{-1}(w)$ of the winsorized value, roughly a proportional change. The second sub-panel drops Equatorial Guinea, the only control with 2003 HIV prevalence above 1\% (3.8\%); it has no gross savings data, so the two sub-panels of Panel A are identical. Sample 1990--2022; controls are the 64 comparison countries valid through 2022. Standard errors clustered by country in parentheses. $^{*}p<0.10$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{***}p<0.01$.\end{minipage}
