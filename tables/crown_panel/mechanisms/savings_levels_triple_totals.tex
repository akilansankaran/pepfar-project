\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}\footnotesize Each cell is the coefficient on HIV prevalence $\times$ PEPFAR $\times$ Post from a two-way fixed effects regression with country and year fixed effects that also includes HIV prevalence $\times$ Post and PEPFAR $\times$ Post; Post = 1 from 2004. HIV prevalence is among ages 15--49 in 2003 (percentage points); PEPFAR is the 0/1 group dummy (All PEPFAR or COP, by column). Unlike the $\%$-of-GDP and per-person versions of this table, outcomes here are country TOTALS: not corrected for either country size or population, so a result that holds up here too is less likely to be an artifact of the chosen denominator. Totals are reconstructed as the per-person series $\times$ UN WPP population, since wdi\_mechanisms\_1990\_2022.csv only carries the per-person values. Adjusted columns add the other 10 baseline (2003) covariates of Crown et al.\ (2023), each interacted with Post. Outcomes from the World Bank WDI (gross savings NY.GNS.ICTR.CD, gross fixed capital formation NE.GDI.FTOT.CD), in current US\$ deflated to 2022 dollars with the US GDP deflator. Winsorized: values outside the 1st--99th percentiles of the full panel set to those percentiles. IHS: $\sinh^{-1}(w)$ of the winsorized value; with country totals spanning several orders of magnitude, this behaves close to a log of levels. The second sub-panel drops Equatorial Guinea, the only control with 2003 HIV prevalence above 1\% (3.8\%). Sample 1990--2022; controls are the 64 comparison countries valid through 2022. Standard errors clustered by country in parentheses. $^{*}p<0.10$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{***}p<0.01$.\end{minipage}
