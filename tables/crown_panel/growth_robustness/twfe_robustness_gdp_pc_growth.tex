Columns (1)--(4): treatment is the 0/1 PEPFAR (or COP) group dummy. Columns (5)--(8): treatment is HIV prevalence among ages 15--49 in 2003 (percentage points), in the same samples, so the coefficient is the post-2004 change in growth per percentage point of baseline HIV prevalence. Adjusted columns add the 11 baseline (2003) covariates of Crown et al.\ (2023), each interacted with Post (columns (6) and (8) leave out HIV prevalence, which is the treatment there). Raw: growth in \% as reported. Winsorized: values outside the 1st--99th percentiles of the full panel set to those percentiles. Winsorized, log: $100\ln(1 + w/100)$ of the winsorized growth rate $w$, the growth rate in log points. Winsorized, IHS: $\sinh^{-1}(w) = \ln(w + \sqrt{w^2+1})$. Panel B drops East, Southeast and South Asia (20 countries, 14 of them PEPFAR recipients and 5 COP) so that the 1997--98 Asian financial crisis cannot drive the before/after comparison. Sample 1990--2022; controls are the 64 comparison countries valid through 2022. Standard errors clustered by country in parentheses. $^{*}p<0.10$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{***}p<0.01$.\end{minipage}
