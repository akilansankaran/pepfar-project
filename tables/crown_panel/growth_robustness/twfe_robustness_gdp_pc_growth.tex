\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}\footnotesize Each cell is the coefficient on treatment $\times$ Post from a separate two-way fixed effects regression with country and year fixed effects; Post = 1 from 2004 for every country. Columns (1)--(4): coefficient on the 0/1 PEPFAR (or COP) group dummy $\times$ Post. Columns (5)--(8): coefficient on HIV prevalence $\times$ PEPFAR $\times$ Post from a regression that also includes HIV prevalence $\times$ Post and PEPFAR $\times$ Post; it is how much more the post-2004 change in growth rises per percentage point of 2003 HIV prevalence (ages 15--49) in the treatment group than in the controls. Adjusted columns add the 11 baseline (2003) covariates of Crown et al.\ (2023), each interacted with Post (columns (6) and (8) leave out HIV prevalence, which is already interacted with Post). Raw: growth in \% as reported. Winsorized: values outside the 1st--99th percentiles of the full panel set to those percentiles. Winsorized, log: $100\ln(1 + w/100)$ of the winsorized growth rate $w$, the growth rate in log points. Winsorized, IHS: $\sinh^{-1}(w) = \ln(w + \sqrt{w^2+1})$. Panel B drops East, Southeast and South Asia (20 countries, 14 of them PEPFAR recipients and 5 COP) so that the 1997--98 Asian financial crisis cannot drive the before/after comparison. Panel C drops Equatorial Guinea, the only control with 2003 HIV prevalence above 1\% (3.8\%), whose oil boom ended just before 2004. Sample 1990--2022; controls are the 64 comparison countries valid through 2022. Standard errors clustered by country in parentheses. $^{*}p<0.10$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{***}p<0.01$.\end{minipage}
