# lit_review

Working bibliography, organised by the component of the project each source informs. Notes on
individual papers go in this folder as one markdown file per paper.

## PEPFAR and macroeconomic outcomes
- Crown et al. (2023). *PLOS ONE* 18: e0289909.
  https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0289909
- Kates et al. (2026).

## Difference-in-differences under staggered adoption
- Sun, L. and Abraham, S. (2021). Estimating dynamic treatment effects in event studies with
  heterogeneous treatment effects. *Journal of Econometrics* 225(2).
- Callaway, B. and Sant'Anna, P. (2021). Difference-in-differences with multiple time periods.
  *Journal of Econometrics* 225(2).
- Goodman-Bacon, A. (2021). Difference-in-differences with variation in treatment timing.
  *Journal of Econometrics* 225(2).

## Mechanisms and mediation
- Avin, C., Shpitser, I. and Pearl, J. (2005). Identifiability of path-specific effects.
  *Proceedings of IJCAI*.

## Control selection
- Belloni, A., Chernozhukov, V. and Hansen, C. (2014). Inference on treatment effects after
  selection among high-dimensional controls. *Review of Economic Studies* 81(2).
- Chernozhukov, V. et al. (2018). Double/debiased machine learning for treatment and structural
  parameters. *Econometrics Journal* 21(1).

## Spillovers and counterfactuals
- Abadie, A., Diamond, A. and Hainmueller, J. (2010). Synthetic control methods for comparative
  case studies. *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 105(490).
- Athey, S. et al. (2021). Matrix completion methods for causal panel data models. *Journal of the
  American Statistical Association* 116(536).
- Brodersen, K. et al. (2015). Inferring causal impact using Bayesian structural time-series
  models. *Annals of Applied Statistics* 9(1).
